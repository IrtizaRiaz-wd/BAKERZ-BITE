OUR BAKING PASSION

Baked With Love, Shared With Happiness.

Bakerz Bite began with a simple belief: the aroma of warm, freshly baked pastry can brighten even the cloudiest day.

Every loaf of sourdough, every butter croissant, and every delicate celebration cake is crafted from scratch in small batches using traditional slow-fermentation techniques and pure farm-fresh ingredients. We never compromise on butter, time, or smiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering, custom cakes, and delivery.

We bake eight artisan categories: Breads & Loaves (Sourdough, Baguette, White, Whole Wheat), Artisan Cakes (Chocolate Fudge, Red Velvet, Fresh Cream, Celebration), Handcrafted Cookies (Chocolate Chip, Shortbread, Oatmeal Raisin, Pistachio), Croissants & Viennoiserie (Croissants, Pain au Chocolat, Cruffins, Éclairs), Gourmet Pies & Tarts (Apple, Blueberry, Lemon Meringue, Pecan), Savory Items (Chicken & Potato Patties, Garlic Bread), Everyday Essentials (Tea Rusks, Buns, Dinner Rolls), and Bakery Merchandise (Aprons, Ceramic Mugs).
